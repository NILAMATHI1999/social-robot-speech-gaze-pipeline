A good conversation with a robot should feel natural. The robot should notice when somebody looks at it, greet that person, and listen without cutting them off. When the person takes a short breath in the middle of a sentence, the robot should keep listening. When the person has really finished, it should answer quickly, because a long silence feels strange. If the robot did not understand something, it should politely ask the person to say it again. At the end of the talk, it should say goodbye and wait for the next person who wants to speak with it.
